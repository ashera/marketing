# Brand & Voice Guide — BlogSeeder

Brand derived from the live site (https://blogger-production-5b98.up.railway.app/). Public CTA domain: **blogseeder.com**.

BlogSeeder — AI blog-post generator with configurable **brand voice, humour, opinions, and stats**. Core promise: content that sounds like *you*, not AI slop. Tagline: *"Plant a topic. Grow a blog."*

## Visual identity
| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Display font | **Plus Jakarta Sans** (headings, overlays) — `brand/fonts/PlusJakartaSans-800.ttf` (also 700), SIL OFL |
| Body / mono | Inter · JetBrains Mono |
| Primary green | `#2d9f77` — CTAs, "Seeder" wordmark, lower-third bars |
| Backgrounds | near-white `#f7fbfb`; pale mint `#eef5f5` |
| Text | near-black on light; **white** on imagery/video |
| Logo / mark | green sprout app icon `brand/blogseeder-icon.png`; wordmark "Blog" (dark) + "Seeder" (green). Hero img: `brand/blogseeder-hero.jpg` |

## Overlay style spec (video)
- White text + soft shadow for legibility on footage; brand accent as a lower-third bar behind the URL/CTA (see frockd's spec for the proven pattern).

## Voice & tone (TODO — confirm)

- Lead with the anti-AI-slop hook; show the configurable knobs (voice/humour/opinions/stats) as the proof; end on the URL CTA.

## Messaging guardrails
- Differentiator: configurable voice/humour/opinions/stats → on-brand, non-generic output.
- Avoid over-claiming; "sounds like you" / "in your voice" framing.
